esque preset reference notices
=============================

The built-in photo looks are modified adaptations for esque, not unmodified
Lightroom presets or measured film-stock simulations. Source parameters are
retuned and camera-specific corrections are omitted. See README.md for the
source-to-preset mapping and changes.
