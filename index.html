esque - open-source photo editor for your browser

A free, web-based Lightroom alternative. Organize and edit your photos in your browser, RAW files included, with reversible adjustments, history and presets.

Photos stay on your device, with no uploads. Organize folders, ratings, flags and collections; exchange edits through Lightroom-compatible XMP; and export JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF or DNG files without overwriting your originals.

The editor requires JavaScript and working WebGPU in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on supported hardware. esque is under active development and is free software under the AGPL-3.0 licence.
